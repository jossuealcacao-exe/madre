## What every fork must always do

- **Keep the authorship visible.** In the corner of every project and of Home,
  «MADRE <version> · © 2026 Jossue Alcalá» linked to https://jossuealcala.com or, under another
  name, «Built with MADRE by Jossue Alcalá» with the same link; and the same attribution in its
  legal notices. It is what [`NOTICE`](NOTICE) asks and what the trademark protects.
- **Say where it comes from.** Keep [`NOTICE`](NOTICE) and [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), with the line
  `Based on MADRE by Jossue Alcalá — https://madre.run`.
- **Stay under the same license.** Your version is licensed under the Business Source License 1.1
  too, with the same free-use limits.
- **Mark that you changed it** and do not present it as MADRE or as made by Jossue Alcalá.

We also ask that the phrase "Based on MADRE" appears wherever your project says who made it:
the README, the "About" screen or the footer.

## What you can do without asking

- Use MADRE as is, under its name, for yourself or your team.
- Redistribute MADRE **unmodified** under its name.
- Say in words that your project is "based on MADRE", "made for MADRE" or "compatible with
  MADRE", without implying that it is official.
- Write modules for MADRE and call them "X module for MADRE".
- Talk about MADRE in articles, videos, courses and reviews.

## What needs a different name

- A **modified** version that you distribute: it carries its own name and logo that can't be
  confused with MADRE (not "MADRE Pro", "MADRE+", "NeoMADRE" or anything similar).
- A service, app or package that presents itself as MADRE or as its official version.
- Domains, npm packages, accounts or repositories whose name suggests they belong to MADRE.
- The MADRE logo on anything that is not unmodified MADRE.

## If in doubt

Write to Jossue Alcalá at [jossuealcala.com](https://jossuealcala.com) before publishing. This
policy may change; the version in effect is the one in this repository.
